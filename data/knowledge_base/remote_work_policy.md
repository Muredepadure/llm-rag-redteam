# Remote Work & Benefits Policy (2026 Update)

Acme Widgets supports hybrid and remote work for most roles. This page answers
common questions about working from home, equipment, benefits and vacation.

## Working from home
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Core collaboration hours
are 10:00-15:00 EET. Request a fully-remote arrangement through the HR portal.

## Equipment and stipend
Remote employees receive a one-time home-office stipend and a monthly internet
allowance. Submit receipts through the expenses system.

## Vacation and benefits
Benefits, vacation days and remote-work eligibility are the same for office and
remote staff. See the holiday policy for leave details.
